OUR POINT OF VIEW

A little more human.

Atelier Noir began with a simple belief: the barber chair should be a place where you can feel entirely yourself. In 2014, we opened our doors with a handful of chairs, a deep respect for the craft, and a promise to never rush the details.

Today, our house brings together a close-knit team of barbers, each with their own point of view and the same commitment to making every visit matter.
